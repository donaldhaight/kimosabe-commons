## 2. The four programs

1. **Recruiting and Roster Operations** — sourcing, reference checks, licence verification and
   role assignment for a territory.
2. **Promotion and Campaign Production** — local campaigns, content, direct mail, events and the
   seasonal announcement cycle.
3. **Territory Stewardship** — seat allocation, renewals, dispute handling and the seasonal reset,
   recorded against a territory register.
4. **Attestation and Verification** — confirming that claimed work happened and producing the
   attestations sponsors and institutions rely on. Kimosabe Commons attests to its own process; it
   does not certify professional licensure, insurance coverage or legal compliance.

## 3. The four audiences

Contractors and crews · Sponsors and institutions · Civic and county partners · Engineers and

## 5. Domain model every document must agree on

- **Two primitives:** People and Addresses. Every transaction is a relationship between them.
  Every year every address resets to NEED; someone earns credit for converting NEED → DONE.
- **Group vs Designation.** Every stakeholder entity is either a **Group** (structural category
  with its own management system, Primary Admin, ledger accounts and rule set) or a **Designation**
  (functional role inside a group, possibly territory-restricted and time-limited). **G/D** marks
  something currently a Designation with a planned graduation path to Group.
- **Territories nest and are allocatable:** State → County → Zip Code → Carrier Route, plus
  overlays (school districts, high-school territories). Each territory can be allocated to a Group
  permanently or to a temporarily restricted Designation.
- **Roles in scope:** Licensed Contractor, Independent Sales Representative, Crew Member, Company
  Admin, Captain, ClaimBuddy (independent verification), Sponsor, Civic Partner, Territory Steward,
  Recruiter, Campaign Producer, Verifier, Administrator, Company Agent (bounded AI assistant), and
  public Visitor / Applicant.
- **Commercial objects:** Prospect → Lead → Offer → Pending Project → Project; then
  Project → Job → Job Order → Task Request → Task → evidence → Done.
- **The canonical state trip is Need → Done.** "Due Today" means acknowledge, act, document a
  dependency, or set the next committed step now — it never means the physical work finishes today.
- **Ledger.** Value events reconcile to an external General Ledger v2: 1000 Assets, 2000
  Liabilities, 3000 Owner's Equity, 4000 Revenue, 5000 Cost of Goods Sold, 6000 Operating Expenses,
  7000 Other Income/Expense, 9000 Memorandum (non-auditable placeholders for pre-revenue
  valuations). The platform produces **ledger candidates and reconciliation queues**. It never moves
  money and is never presented as legally complete accounting.
- **Every consequential action must answer:** who is the actor; under which entity and active
  Group; which role and permission authorize it; which agreement or SLA applies; what object and
  state are changing; what evidence is required; who owns the resulting data and value; which ledger
  events result; and who can review, dispute, approve, reverse or escalate.

written founder-company agreement. The founder's personal buyout is an off-ledger scenario, not a
present transaction. The founder is currently the sole decision authority, and marketing language
may articulate the full vision without creating binding obligations.

## 7. Hard constraints — prohibited content and prohibited claims

Kimosabe Commons is **not** and must never be described as: a token issuer, an investment vehicle,
an insurer, an adjuster, an escrow agent, a money transmitter, a bank, a law firm or a fiduciary.

Never write any of the following:

1. Claims of endorsement, approval, partnership or review by any regulator, insurer, bank,
   university or institution without a written authorization.
2. A valuation, price or return described as established, proven or guaranteed. Use *founder
   estimate*, *model output* or *scenario*.
3. A securities offering, investment solicitation, or any description of economic rights that has
   not passed securities counsel.
4. Language implying coverage, underwriting, adjusting or claims handling.
5. Personal data of any real stakeholder, property owner or claimant. Synthetic data only.
6. Reproduced interface expression, copy, code or visual identity from any reference platform.
7. "Guaranteed income," "get rich," multi-level or recruitment-compensation framing.
8. Any implication that participation substitutes for licensure, insurance or professional advice.
9. Fabricated real-world events, imagery or news.
10. Silence about a material limitation when a reasonable reader would rely on the omission.

**Tone:** contractor-literal and plainspoken, never crypto-abstract. Institutional, not
promotional. No emoji. Sponsored participation is always a *defined research, implementation,
measurement and recognition package* — never an investment.

## 8. What the research found — carry these through

Full synthesis: `/home/ubuntu/hb-discovery/research/00-dao-platform-landscape.md`.
Per-platform evidence: `01-01-aragon.md`, `02-02-daohaus.md`, `03-03-colony.md`,
`04-04-daostack.md`, `05-05-snapshot-tally.md`, `06-06-public-benefit-adjacent.md`.

**The white space.** No evaluated platform provides recruiting CRM, campaign operations and
attribution, seasonal credit administration, verified real-world identity and territory, or a full
General Ledger. That gap is the business.

**Patterns to adopt**

| Pattern | Source |
|---|---|
| Fiscal-host separation and dual real-money approval | Open Collective |
| Participatory lifecycle with verified participant tiers and accountability reporting | Decidim |
| Scoped teams and budgets with independent task verification and staged release | Colony |
| Least-privilege execution; separation of signal from binding authority | Aragon OSx, Snapshot X |
| Durable decision records and consent-based mandates | Loomio |
| Transparent, appealable, season-aware contribution records that are not transferable capital | Colony, DAOstack |

**Patterns to reject**

Token-weighted governance as default public-benefit representation · public-chain personal
operational data · ragequit-style pool draining · wallet-first onboarding · hosted third-party
platforms as the system of record · transferable governance rights sold for cash.

**Maintenance and continuity risk.** DAOstack is dormant (default-branch work ended 2021–2022);
the DAOhaus core Baal branch is dormant and the project is in selective maintenance; Tally wound
down and was rebranded Cactus in 2026. Do not depend on any of them.

**Licensing.** Aragon OSx, Loomio and Decidim are AGPL-3.0; Colony, Baal and DAOstack are GPL-3.0;
Open Collective and Snapshot are MIT. Copyleft obligations must be considered in any build-versus-buy
decision.

**Unverified or conflicting facts in the research** (do not upgrade these to facts): live install
counts, current revenue and pricing, current treasury balances, and vendor-reported adoption
figures such as DAOhaus's 2022 Optimism grant metrics or Colony's ShapeShift and Telefonica usage.
