# Project Instruction Manual

**Engagement:** Research the top five open-source DAO management platforms, then propose, specify, and stand up a public benefit business that performs marketing, recruiting, promotion, community management, and governance operations for the Kimosabe App and the Human Blockchain.

**Governing principle:** *Once and done.* Every structural decision that governs this engagement is defined here, up front, so that no later document has to renegotiate its own shape. If a downstream artifact contradicts this manual, the manual wins unless the founder explicitly amends it in the Change Log (Section 12).

---

## 1. Preface — Why This Manual Exists

The founder has spent more than twenty years developing one system across many tools, companies, drafts, and media. That work has produced an enormous supply of intention and very little of it is committed to a single, legible operating structure. Every new session run by a human or an agent therefore re-derives the same decisions and drifts.

This manual ends that drift for this engagement. It fixes:

1. **What is being produced** — the complete artifact set, in order, with a definition of done for each.
2. **How it is produced** — the research method, the evidence discipline, the independence boundary.
3. **What it looks like** — the naming, voice, visual identity, and document template for every deliverable.
4. **What it will never do** — the prohibited-content and prohibited-claim register.

This is not a summary of the work. It is the constitution of the work.

## 2. Introduction — The Engagement in Plain Language

The founder asked one question with four deliverables behind it:

> **Research the top five open-source DAO management platforms and propose a startup — a public benefit business — including a business plan, an SRS, a PRD, and a website that we can set up and execute to be the marketing, recruiting, promotion, management, and operations engine for the Kimosabe App and the Human Blockchain.**

Read plainly, the request contains four distinct jobs:

| # | Job | Output | Owner in this engagement |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Discover** what the open-source DAO market has already solved | Evidence-led competitive brief | Research fan-out (5 platforms + synthesis) |
| 2 | **Propose** a lawful, funded, public-benefit company | Business plan + entity architecture | Business plan author |
| 3 | **Specify** the software that company will run on | SRS + PRD (traceable, non-functional, risk-reviewed) | Product/specification author |
| 4 | **Stand up** the public face of the company | A live, published website | Web build |

These are not four documents. They are **one system described at four altitudes**: market, money, machine, and door.

### The one-sentence thesis

> A public benefit corporation that turns the Human Blockchain's stakeholder taxonomy, seasonal cycle, and General Ledger into a **recruiting, promotion, and stewardship engine** — so that engineers, contractors, captains, sponsors, and civic partners can be found, onboarded, verified, activated, and retained at county scale without the founder acting as human middleware.

### What "public benefit" means here, concretely

The entity is chartered so that its chartered public benefit is *measurable and auditable*, not decorative. The chartered benefit is:

> **Increasing the number of verified, address-anchored, lawfully organized participants who can convert a documented community Need into a documented, evidenced Done — measured as verified stakeholder activations, evidenced completions, and retained participation per territory per season.**

That wording is a charter, a KPI definition, and a marketing claim simultaneously. It is written to be defensible in a benefit report under Delaware public benefit corporation law.

### 4.5 One-paragraph summary (250 words — for the business plan executive summary)

> The Human Blockchain describes a market architecture in which a domain or relationship originates a Lead, verified identity and role determine who may act, accepted work becomes evidenced completion, and a ledger records value. That architecture does not recruit itself. Its bottleneck is the human founder acting as middleware between an idea, a company, a territory, a contractor, and a transaction. Kimosabe Commons, PBC exists to remove that bottleneck by industrializing four functions: **recruiting** the participants a territory needs, **promoting** the opportunity to those participants and to the institutions that fund them, **managing** the roster, roles, territories, and seasonal obligations, and **certifying** that the work happened. The company is chartered for a measurable public benefit — increasing verified, address-anchored participants who can convert a documented Need into evidenced Done. Revenue comes from four disclosed streams: sponsor and founding-seat programs, territory and seat subscription, campaign and content production services, and attestation and verification services. It is deliberately not a token issuer, not an investment vehicle, not an insurer, and not a lobbyist. Its immediate job is small and winnable: run one complete recruiting-and-activation season in a bounded set of territories, publish the measured result, and use the result to raise the next season.

## 5. Voice, Style, and Copy Rules

1. **Contractor-literal, never crypto-abstract.** Say *"the roster for your county,"* not *"a permissionless coordination primitive."*
2. **Named roles get capital letters.** Stakeholder Group, Designation, Territory, Season, Lead, Offer, Project, Job, Task, Need, Done, ClaimCoin, JoeBack. This mirrors the existing OKF glossary and prevents drift.
3. **No unsupported performance claims.** Never publish a valuation, conversion rate, partnership, or endorsement that is not evidenced in the registers. Estimates are labelled *"founder estimate"* or *"model output."*
4. **No securities language.** Never *invest, ROI, equity, return, token sale, yield, appreciation.* Sponsorship is sold as a **defined research, implementation, measurement, and recognition package**.
5. **No insurance language.** Never imply coverage, underwriting, adjusting, or claims handling. Refer to licensed third parties.
6. **Accessibility in copy:** plain-language summary at the top of any document longer than two pages. Reading level target: grade 9 for public pages, grade 12 for business documents.
7. **Agent attribution:** machine-authored passages are marked in front-matter (`prepared_by`), never hidden.

## 6. Image and Visual Usage Policy

Images carry meaning in this ecosystem; they are not decoration. Rules:

1. **No fabricated real people.** Never generate a lookalike of a named founder, official, or partner. Use a supplied photo or omit the image.
2. **No fabricated evidence.** Never generate an image implying a real event, endorsement, site installation, or news coverage that did not occur. Illustrative imagery must be captioned as illustrative.
3. **Original assets only.** Brand illustrations, map textures, and diagrams are original work for this engagement, informed by — never copied from — a reference platform's interface or identity.
4. **Persona imagery.** Human figures representing stakeholders are composite and anonymized: no readable logo, license plate, address, invoice, or claim document.
5. **Required image set for the website** (planned placements, generated at build time):
   - Hero: a border-anchored territory map rendered as an abstract field of address nodes, warm and civic, not "cyber."
   - Section: a seasonal clock diagram (Pre-Season, Season, Cool-Down, Dispute, Reset).
   - Section: a verified roster concept — a roster card showing role, territory, license status, and season completion, with all data synthetic.
   - Section: the funnel — discovery to activation to evidenced Done.
   - Sustainability/benefit image: a published benefit report page concept.
6. **Diagram policy:** use D2 for architecture and data-flow; Mermaid for sequences, flows, and dependency graphs; render both to PNG/SVG and keep the source file beside the render.
7. **Palette and accessibility:** every image and diagram must survive greyscale and pass contrast checks for embedded text.

## 7. Design System for the Website

**Design movement:** *Civic Ledger* — the visual language of a county records office, a union hall, and a well-kept field notebook, translated to screen. Not cyberpunk, not fintech dark-mode.

**Core principles**
1. **Ledger honesty** — visible rules, visible line items, visible numbers. Nothing floats free of a source.
2. **Territory warmth** — maps, borders, seasons, and land rendered with human warmth rather than surveillance aesthetics.
3. **Roster clarity** — the human is always identifiable as a role with authority, never as a metric.
4. **Quiet authority** — the site reads like an institution that already exists.

**Color philosophy**

| Token | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `--ink` | `#1B2A2F` | Record ink — the base text color |
| `--paper` | `#F6F2E9` | Ledger paper, warm, not white |
| `--field` | `#2F6B4F` | Territory green — the ownable signature color |
| `--brass` | `#B0801F` | Brass rule, seal, certification accent |
| `--clay` | `#9C4A2F` | Attention, deadlines, cool-down warnings |
| `--sky` | `#4A6E8A` | Institutional, sponsor-facing, reference |

**Layout paradigm:** a two-column ledger spine — a narrow, sticky left rail containing section numbers and rule marks, and a wide right field containing content. Sections are separated by hairlines and brass numerals, not by cards floating on gradients. Asymmetry is deliberate: content blocks begin at the left rail and extend to varying measures.

**Signature elements**
1. **Brass rule marks** — short horizontal rules with a numeral, used as section markers and as list bullets.
2. **The address field** — a faint grid of small squares (address nodes) used as a background texture in hero and footer bands; individual nodes light up on scroll.
3. **The season band** — a horizontal segmented bar representing the five phases of the seasonal clock, reused as a progress indicator across the site.

**Interaction philosophy:** restraint. Hover reveals a source or a role definition rather than an animation flourish. Every interactive element must be keyboard operable. Motion exists to show sequence (the season clock, the funnel), never to decorate.

**Animation:** fade-and-rise on section entry (200–320 ms, ease-out, 12 px translate); season-band fill animation tied to scroll position; brass rules drawing in from left over 240 ms; address nodes breathing at very low amplitude (opacity 0.35→0.55 over 4 s) only in the hero, disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

**Typography system**

- Display: **Fraunces** (or **Source Serif 4**) — institutional serif with warmth; used for the wordmark, section numerals, and headlines.
- Text: **Inter** — neutral, legible, excellent at small sizes for rosters and tables.
- Data/mono: **JetBrains Mono** or **IBM Plex Mono** — used for territory codes, ledger line items, and identifiers.
- Hierarchy: display 56/64, h1 40/48, h2 30/38, h3 22/30, body 17/28, data 14/22, caption 13/20.

**Brand essence:** *The recruiting and stewardship institution for a verified map of people and addresses.* Personality: **accountable, plainspoken, territorial.**

**Brand voice examples**
- Headline: *"A county is not a market. It is a roster waiting to be written."*
- CTA: *"Claim your territory for Season 1."*
- (Rejected filler: "Welcome to our website," "Get started today.")

**Wordmark & logo concept:** the word *KIMOSABE* set in Fraunces with tight tracking, preceded by a **mark** that reads two ways — a brass ledger rule crossed by a short vertical tick, forming both the numeral *1* and the letter *I*, and, rotated, a surveyor's stake on a property line. It sits inside a thin brass square on paper, like a seal.

**Signature brand color:** `--field` `#2F6B4F` (Territory Green). Unmistakably this brand; used for the mark, primary buttons, and the season band.

## 8. Research Method and Evidence Discipline

This engagement follows an evidence-led method. The full apparatus lives in `research/07-evidence-ledger.md`; the rules are:

1. **Start from the decision.** The decision this research informs is: *what should Kimosabe Commons build, buy, or deliberately refuse to build?*
2. **One dedicated research unit per platform.** No bundling. Five platforms, five reports, one synthesis.
3. **Comparison set:** Aragon (Aragon OSx / Aragon App), DAOhaus (Moloch v3 / Baal), Colony, DAOstack (Alchemy/Arc), Snapshot + Tally, plus one public-benefit and civic adjacent set (Open Collective, Loomio, Decidim).
4. **Label every material claim:** `OBSERVED` (seen in a primary source), `REPORTED` (stated by a credible third party), `INFERRED` (reasoned from evidence), `ASSUMED` (unverified working assumption), `DECIDED` (a founder or engagement decision).
5. **Record provenance:** URL, access date, confidence, restriction.
6. **Use authorized access only.** Public repositories, public documentation, public pricing pages, ordinary product access. No bypassing controls, no unauthorized accounts, no scraping behind login.
7. **Comparative, not imitative.** Feature matching is an input to a strategic decision, never a build mandate. Every material learning is dispositioned as **ADOPT / ADAPT / REJECT / DEFER / VALIDATE**.
8. **Independent design boundary.** No platform's interface expression, copy, visual identity, or source code is reproduced. Implementations are independently authored from a functional brief.

## 9. Specification Standard

Every requirements document in this engagement uses the same skeleton, so that any reader can find any part of any document:

```
1. Purpose and scope
2. Definitions and glossary delta
3. Actors, groups, and designations
4. Functional requirements   (FR-x.y, each with: statement, rationale, evidence link, priority, acceptance criteria)
5. Non-functional requirements (NFR-x.y, each with: measurable target, verification method)
6. Data, events, and ledger effects
7. Interfaces and integrations
8. Roles and permissions
9. Failure, dispute, and exception behavior
10. Assumption tests and open questions
11. Decision records (ADR-x)
12. Traceability matrix
```

**Requirement ID grammar:** `FR-<domain>-<n>` (e.g. `FR-RECRUIT-04`), `NFR-<quality>-<n>` (e.g. `NFR-A11Y-02`), `ADR-<n>`, `RISK-<n>`, `EV-<n>` (evidence).

**Priority scale:** `P0` required for the first operating season · `P1` required for scale · `P2` desired · `P3` deferred with a named trigger.

**Non-functional targets for this engagement:**

| Quality | Target |
|---|---|
| Accessibility | WCAG 2.2 AA on all public pages and core flows; keyboard-complete; reduced-motion honored |
| Performance | LCP < 2.0 s on 4G mobile for public pages; API p95 < 400 ms for read paths |
| Authorization | Server-side enforcement on every mutation; deny-by-default; privileged actions audited |
| Privacy | Data minimization; no policyholder or claim data in marketing systems; consent records retained |
| Reliability | 99.5 % monthly availability target for Season 1; documented recovery path; no irreversible automation |
| Auditability | Every consequential action answers actor, entity, group, role, authority, object, evidence, ledger effect |
| Internationalization | U.S. English first; all copy externalized for later locale work |
| Compliance posture | No money movement, no securities offer, no insurance function without named licensed provider and counsel sign-off |

## 10. Prohibited Content and Prohibited Claims Register

The following are prohibited in every artifact, page, deck, or campaign produced under this engagement:

1. Claims of endorsement, approval, partnership, or review by any regulator, insurer, bank, university, or institution unless a written authorization exists.
2. Any statement that a valuation, price, or return is *established*, *proven*, or *guaranteed*. Use *founder estimate*, *model output*, *scenario*.
3. Any securities offering, investment solicitation, or description of economic rights that has not passed securities counsel.
4. Any description of the PBC as an insurer, adjuster, escrow agent, money transmitter, bank, law firm, or fiduciary.
5. Personal data of any real stakeholder, property owner, or claimant in any public or demonstration environment. Synthetic data only.
6. Reproduced interface expression, copy, or visual identity from any reference platform.
7. "Guaranteed income," "get rich," multi-level or recruitment-compensation framing of any kind.
8. Any implication that participation in the ecosystem is a substitute for licensure, insurance, or professional advice.
9. Fabricated real-world events, imagery, or news.
10. Silence about a material limitation when a reasonable reader would rely on the omission.

## 11. Working Protocols

1. **Never delete, always append.** Registers grow; documents iterate. Superseded content is moved, dated, and preserved.
2. **Registers before documents.** Update `research/07-evidence-ledger.md` and the decisions register before rewriting a dependent document.
3. **Multi-pass review.** Every material artifact is read at least twice through different lenses: Founder → Sponsor/Investor → Regulatory/Compliance → Technical → Narrative.
4. **Redline for information, rewrite for commit.** Show contested changes as redlines; commit clean text.
5. **Open questions are logged, not buried.** Every artifact ends with an explicit open-questions block.
6. **One file, one folder.** Consolidated artifacts over scattered fragments.
7. **Assumption marking.** When a fact is unavailable and the work must continue, state the assumption in-line and in the register.
8. **Human authority is explicit.** No artifact produced here binds the founder, the LLC, or any future entity. It is a proposal for a decision.

## 14. Closing

This engagement ends when four things are true:

1. Five open-source DAO management platforms are researched to an evidence standard, with a synthesis that states plainly what to adopt and what to refuse.
2. A public benefit corporation exists on paper — name, charter benefit, revenue model, unit economics, governance, and first-season plan — that a competent advisor could critique without first having to ask what it is.
3. The software that company runs on is specified precisely enough that an engineering team could build the first operating season without inventing the product.
4. A real website is live, carrying the real name, the real offer, and the real funnel.

The manual closes with the founder's own governing posture, restated for this company:

> **Build one complete loop before scaling. Present the real problem, the proposed control system, the evidence, the assumptions, and the risks early enough that credible institutions can challenge the design before regulated activity begins. Reward verified useful work, not attention.**
